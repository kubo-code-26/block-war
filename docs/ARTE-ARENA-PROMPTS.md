## Prompts usados

### Fundição

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: finished background artwork for the Block War mobile puzzle arena, a single portrait 2:3 image, no UI. Cinematic physically based realistic 3D industrial foundry arena: massive brushed steel columns, dark forged iron, restrained molten amber channels along the side edges, light smoke, subtle copper sparks. A believable premium game environment, strong depth, tactile materials and realistic lighting, not pixel art, not cartoon, no Tetris shapes. Composition: symmetrical architectural frame, detailed side edges and top, central lower two-thirds quiet dark charcoal space with very low contrast so a 10x20 puzzle board can be overlaid legibly. No characters, no text, no logos, no watermark, no interface. Rich black and graphite with amber highlights, original environment.

### Catedral de gelo

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: finished portrait 2:3 background artwork for a premium mobile puzzle arena. Create a photorealistic 3D ice cathedral: translucent blue ice columns and carved crystalline arches on both sides, frost, fine realistic cracks, restrained turquoise light, a dark reflective frozen floor. Symmetrical architectural frame with most fine details at the edges and top; central lower two-thirds must remain quiet dark navy, very low contrast, suitable for overlaying a clearly readable 10x20 block puzzle board. Tactile realistic materials and convincing cinematic illumination, subtle snow mist, no characters, no cartoon, no pixel art, no Tetris pieces, no words, no logos, no watermark, no UI. Original environment, elegant rather than busy.

### Caverna de cristais

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: finished portrait 2:3 background artwork for Block War mobile puzzle arena. Premium photorealistic 3D subterranean crystal sanctuary, enormous faceted amethyst and smoky quartz formations framing both edges and top like natural architecture, realistic refraction and mineral striations, subtle indigo and restrained magenta caustic reflections on a dark stone floor. Central lower two-thirds quiet, very dark low-contrast navy-purple negative space for a readable 10x20 puzzle board overlay. Sophisticated physically based materials and cinematic lighting, original environment, no people, no cartoon, no pixel art, no Tetris shapes, no UI, no text, no logos, no watermark. Elegant, realistic rather than oversaturated neon.

### Atlas de materiais

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: one production game material texture atlas, landscape 4:3, exactly 4 equal-width columns and 3 equal-height rows, 12 perfectly aligned square tiles, edge-to-edge with no gutters, no margins, no text or labels. Each tile is a photorealistic physically based material viewed straight-on, flat orthographic, with tactile microtexture and restrained studio light, not a UI button and not an icon. Ordered tiles left-to-right top-to-bottom: row 1 brushed stainless steel with hairline scratches; satin titanium with faint violet iridescence; glossy black obsidian with tiny warm orange fissures; dark volcanic crust with molten amber cracks. Row 2 smoky teal tempered glass with reflections; pale blue clear ice with natural fractures and trapped bubbles; faceted violet amethyst quartz with visible triangular crystal planes; aged copper with a little green patina. Row 3 ivory marble with fine natural gray-gold veins; realistic dark walnut wood grain; gunmetal gray metal plate with dark horizontal grooves; rough dark basalt stone. All surfaces fill their square fully, same frontal orientation and consistent restrained top-left lighting. No objects, no characters, no borders between tiles, no logos, no watermark, no pixel art, no cartoon.
